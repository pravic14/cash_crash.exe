---
id: account-locked
title: Account will be locked
patterns:
  - account will be locked
  - verify now
  - suspended within
  - confirm your identity now
red_flags:
  - A deadline of minutes to verify
  - The link is the only way they offer
bank_never_asks:
  - Verify your identity through a link in a text to stop a lock
advice: Open the bank's app yourself. A lock is never lifted by a link in a message.
---

The threat of a locked account is there to stop the customer thinking. The link is the trap.
