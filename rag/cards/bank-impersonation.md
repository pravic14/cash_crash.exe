---
id: bank-impersonation
title: Bank impersonation
patterns:
  - fraud team
  - this is your bank
  - your bank is calling
  - unusual activity on your account
red_flags:
  - Caller claims to be the bank fraud team
  - They already know some of the customer's details
bank_never_asks:
  - Call the number in the text back and confirm a code
  - Move money while still on the phone
advice: Hang up and call the number on the back of the bank card.
---

A genuine fraud team will not rush a customer into a transfer during the same call.
