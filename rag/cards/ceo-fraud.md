---
id: ceo-fraud
title: Boss or CEO fraud
patterns:
  - the chief executive
  - urgent wire
  - do not discuss this
  - i need you to pay this today
red_flags:
  - A senior person demands a secret same-day transfer
  - The request arrives by email or message, not the usual process
bank_never_asks:
  - Bypass the normal payment checks because a director is in a hurry
advice: Confirm the request with the person face to face or on a known number.
---

The pressure and the secrecy are the scam. Real urgent payments still go through the normal checks.
