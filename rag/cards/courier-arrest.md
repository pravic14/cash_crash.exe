---
id: courier-arrest
title: Courier collects cash or cards
patterns:
  - courier will collect
  - hand the cash to the driver
  - we are sending someone to collect
red_flags:
  - A driver is coming to the house to collect cash or cards
  - The caller stays on the phone until the courier arrives
bank_never_asks:
  - Give cash, cards, or a PIN to a courier
advice: Do not hand anything to a driver. Hang up and call the bank on the number on your card.
---

This is the in-person ending of a safe-account or impersonation call. Nobody genuine is sent to collect.
