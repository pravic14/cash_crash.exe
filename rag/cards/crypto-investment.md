---
id: crypto-investment
title: Crypto or investment scam
patterns:
  - guaranteed return
  - crypto wallet
  - investment opportunity
  - your profits are locked
red_flags:
  - Returns are guaranteed
  - A further payment is required to withdraw profits
bank_never_asks:
  - Send more money to unlock an investment profit
advice: You cannot unlock a profit by paying again. Stop and talk to the bank.
---

The first payment looks like an investment. Later payments are to release money that is not there.
