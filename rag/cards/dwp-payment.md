---
id: dwp-payment
title: DWP or cost-of-living payment scam
patterns:
  - cost of living payment
  - dwp
  - benefits payment waiting
red_flags:
  - A benefit payment is waiting if the customer clicks or pays a fee
  - The sender claims to be DWP by text
bank_never_asks:
  - Pay a fee to release a benefit
advice: Benefit payments are not released by text. Check through the official government account.
---

These copy real cost-of-living payments and then ask for details or a small fee.
