---
id: family-emergency
title: Family emergency scam
patterns:
  - your grandchild
  - been in an accident
  - needs bail
  - do not tell mum
red_flags:
  - A relative is supposedly hurt or arrested
  - The caller says not to check with the rest of the family
bank_never_asks:
  - Send bail or medical money before you have spoken to the relative yourself
advice: Hang up and call that relative on the number you already have.
---

These calls copy a grandchild's or child's situation and demand a fast transfer.
