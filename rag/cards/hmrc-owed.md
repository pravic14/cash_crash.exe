---
id: hmrc-owed
title: HMRC unpaid-tax scam
patterns:
  - unpaid tax
  - outstanding tax
  - hmrc warrant
  - tax is overdue
red_flags:
  - Threat of arrest or a warrant over tax
  - Demand to pay immediately to a new account
bank_never_asks:
  - Pay a tax bill to an account named in a text message
advice: HMRC does not arrest people by text. Use the contact details on gov.uk, not the message.
---

Threatening unpaid-tax messages are impersonation. The account they name is not HMRC.
