---
id: hmrc-refund
title: HMRC tax refund scam
patterns:
  - tax refund
  - hmrc owes
  - rebate is waiting
red_flags:
  - A text or email says HMRC owes a refund
  - It links to a page that asks for card details
bank_never_asks:
  - HMRC does not send refund texts that ask you to move money
advice: Check any tax refund by signing in at gov.uk yourself. Do not use the link in the message.
---

Refund texts that pretend to be HMRC are a common way to steal card details or push a payment.
