---
id: invoice-change
title: Invoice or payee-detail change
patterns:
  - new bank details
  - updated invoice
  - sort code has changed
  - please use this account instead
red_flags:
  - A supplier's bank details change by email
  - The new account is urgent and just for this invoice
bank_never_asks:
  - Change a payee's account details because an email said so
advice: Phone the supplier on a number you already had, not the number in the email.
---

Invoice-redirection fraud swaps the destination account on a payment the customer meant to make.
