---
id: job-fee
title: Job advance-fee scam
patterns:
  - training fee
  - equipment fee
  - pay before you start
  - job offer fee
red_flags:
  - A job requires payment before the first wage
  - The offer arrived by message and the company is vague
bank_never_asks:
  - Pay a fee to start a job or to receive equipment
advice: A real employer does not charge you to start. Stop contact and do not pay.
---

The role is the bait. The fee is the scam, and further fees usually follow.
