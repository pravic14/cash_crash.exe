---
id: mule-job
title: Money-mule recruitment
patterns:
  - receive money and forward
  - keep a commission
  - use your account to receive
  - work from home transfers
red_flags:
  - A job is mostly receiving money and sending it on
  - They offer a cut for the use of the account
bank_never_asks:
  - Let someone else pay into your account and pass it on
advice: Do not accept the money. Receiving it and sending it on can make you part of the fraud.
---

Mule recruits are told the money is legitimate. It is usually the proceeds of other scams.
