---
id: parcel-fee
title: Parcel delivery fee scam
patterns:
  - royal mail
  - dpd
  - customs fee
  - parcel is waiting
  - redelivery fee
red_flags:
  - A small fee is demanded to release a parcel the customer was not expecting
  - The link asks for full card details
bank_never_asks:
  - Pay a customs fee from a text to receive a parcel
advice: Track parcels only on the courier's own site. A few pounds is still a card-detail theft.
---

Missed-delivery texts are used to steal card details, and sometimes to start a larger payment scam.
