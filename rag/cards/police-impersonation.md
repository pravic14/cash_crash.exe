patterns:
  - action fraud
  - this is the police
  - undercover operation
  - do not tell the bank
red_flags:
  - Caller says they are the police and the bank is in on it
  - They tell the customer to keep the call secret
bank_never_asks:
  - Keep a transfer secret from the bank
  - Help an undercover operation by moving money
advice: The police will not ask you to move money or to lie to your bank.
---

Secrecy plus a transfer is the pattern. End the call and use a number you look up yourself.
