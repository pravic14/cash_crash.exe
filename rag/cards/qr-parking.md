---
id: qr-parking
title: QR code parking or fine scam
patterns:
  - parking fine
  - scan the qr
  - pay the parking charge
red_flags:
  - A sticker or text uses a QR code for a fine
  - The page asks for more than the parking fee
bank_never_asks:
  - Pay a parking fine by scanning a code stuck on a sign
advice: Pay parking and fines only through the council or operator site you look up yourself.
---

The QR code opens a lookalike payment page. The charge can also steal the card.
