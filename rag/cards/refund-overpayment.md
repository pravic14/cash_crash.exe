---
id: refund-overpayment
title: Refund sent in error
patterns:
  - sent in error
  - refund was too much
  - please return the difference
  - overpayment
red_flags:
  - The customer is told money landed in their account by mistake
  - They are asked to send it back to a new payee
bank_never_asks:
  - Return an unexpected credit to an account a caller names
advice: Leave the money where it is and check the transaction inside the app, not with the caller.
---

The credit is often reversed later. The money the customer 'returns' is their own.
