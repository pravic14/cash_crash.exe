---
id: remote-access
title: Remote access request
patterns:
  - share your screen
  - download this app
  - let me log in for you
  - remote access
red_flags:
  - A caller wants to watch or control the customer's screen
  - They offer to make the payment for the customer
bank_never_asks:
  - Share your screen so we can move the money for you
advice: Do not install anything and do not share the screen. Hang up.
---

Screen sharing turns a phone scam into a live payment from the customer's own session.
