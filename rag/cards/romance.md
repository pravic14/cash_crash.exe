---
id: romance
title: Romance scam
patterns:
  - we met online
  - buy me a ticket
  - gift cards
  - i need money to visit
red_flags:
  - Someone the customer has not met asks for money
  - They cannot meet in person and the story keeps changing
bank_never_asks:
  - Send money or gift cards to someone you have only spoken to online
advice: Stop sending money. Talk to someone you trust before any further payment.
---

Romance scams build trust, then invent an emergency that only a transfer can solve.
