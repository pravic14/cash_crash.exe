---
id: safe-account
title: Safe-account scam
patterns:
  - safe account
  - move your money
  - account is compromised
red_flags:
  - Caller or text claims to be the bank, police, or HMRC
  - They tell the customer to move money to a new "safe" account
  - They create urgency or claim the account is already under attack
bank_never_asks:
  - Move your money to a different account to keep it safe
  - Share a one-time code, or install remote-access software
advice: Hang up. Call the number on the back of the bank card. A real bank will not ask you to move money to a safe account.
---

A bank, the police, or HMRC will never ask a customer to transfer money to a "safe account" they name. The destination account is controlled by the scammer.
